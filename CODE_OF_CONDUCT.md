# Community standards

We want Aster to be useful to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and explain assumptions.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or expose private information.
- Do not spam, fabricate benchmark claims, or solicit artificial engagement.
- Credit contributors and upstream work.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, request changes, or restrict participation. Apply these measures proportionately and explain decisions when doing so is safe.

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers through an available private channel. Do not publish private evidence or sensitive information in an issue. A dedicated private contact must be configured before public launch.
